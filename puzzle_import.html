🧩 Puzzle Tracker — Instagram Import

This tool imports your 403 Instagram puzzles into the Puzzle Tracker Google Sheet and uploads the photos.

Step 1: Sign in as owner

Step 2: Import puzzle data (text only — 403 rows)

Appends all 403 rows to your Sheet. Photos are handled in Step 3.

Step 3: Upload photos

Select your Instagram export folder(s), then click Upload. The tool will match each row to its photo and upload it automatically.

The main export folder should contain a media/ subfolder. The 2nd export folder is the separate zip you uploaded later (also contains media/).
